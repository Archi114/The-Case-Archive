11:45 PM — Blackwood Mansion, London

It was a cold November night in London. At exactly 11:45 PM, Inspector Holmes received an urgent message from Blackwood Mansion. A wealthy businessman, Mr. Edward Blackwood, had been found unconscious in his study, and his priceless diamond necklace had disappeared.

The strange part? The study was locked from the inside. All the windows were shut, the fireplace was too narrow for anyone to enter, and the only key was lying beside the desk.

Three people were inside the mansion that night: Mrs. Blackwood, who claimed she was reading in her bedroom; Thomas, the butler, who said he was preparing tea in the kitchen; and Clara, Mr. Blackwood's niece, who claimed she was playing the piano in the drawing room.

───────── ✦ ─────────

Three people. Three different stories. One missing necklace. One locked room. The truth is buried somewhere between the clues. Will you be able to solve the mystery?

✦ A SECRET FROM THE PAST ✦

THE TRUTH BEHIND THE MISSING NECKLACE

──────── ❖ ────────

Just as I began to believe that the mystery was confined to the locked study, a discovery changed everything. The diamond necklace was not simply stolen—it had been hidden somewhere inside Blackwood Mansion long before that mysterious night.

A closer examination of the old photograph from 1998 revealed a familiar detail: the necklace was visible around the neck of a woman who was never mentioned in the family records. Why had Mr. Blackwood kept this secret for so many years?

And why did someone suddenly want the necklace to disappear? Perhaps the real mystery was never about who stole a priceless jewel, but about the secret it carried. The necklace may be missing, but its truth is about to be uncovered.
